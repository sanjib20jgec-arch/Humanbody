## 3. Features
**3D**: homologous forelimbs (human arm, bat wing, whale flipper, cat leg) with colour-matched bones — reuse atlas human skeleton for the human arm; analogous organs (bird vs insect wing); vestigial structures on the atlas (appendix, coccyx, ear muscles); fossil-layer strata block (SectionSlicer reused) showing older-deeper ordering; skull comparison (hominin series, schematic, sourced proportions).
**Animations (≤ 30 s)**: Miller–Urey apparatus; peppered-moth (industrial melanism) selection; Darwin's finches adaptive radiation map; giraffe neck — Lamarck vs Darwin explanation side-by-side (with misconception flag); speciation by geographic isolation.
**Simulations**: (1) **Natural selection lab** — population of organisms with heritable trait variation, environment/predator settings, generations graph; (2) **Hardy–Weinberg lab** (NEET) — allele frequencies p, q, check equilibrium, add selection / drift / migration / mutation; (3) **Genetic drift lab** — small vs large population, founder effect; (4) Adaptation explorer — habitat (desert, aquatic, polar, arboreal) → structural/physiological/behavioural adaptations cards.
**Missions**: "Be Darwin on the Galápagos" — collect finch beak data, infer selection.
**Assessment**: evidence-type classification, homologous vs analogous sorting, Lamarck vs Darwin assertion–reason, Hardy–Weinberg numericals (NEET).
**Engagement**: curiosity hook "Why do whales have finger bones?"; benchmark PhET *Natural Selection* (CC BY 4.0).

## 4. Accuracy specifics
- Evolution is not "progress toward humans"; no ladder imagery (use branching trees).
- "Humans evolved from monkeys" misconception → common ancestor explanation.
- Lamarckism presented as historical, falsified hypothesis.
- "Survival of the fittest" = reproductive success, not strength.
- Hardy–Weinberg assumptions listed explicitly; Miller–Urey framed as a historical experiment with modern caveats.
