## 3. Features
**3D**: interactive ecosystem diorama (pond / forest / Sundarbans mangrove) with tappable producers, consumers, decomposers; nitrogen cycle 3D loop (atmosphere → lightning / Rhizobium root nodules → soil → plants → animals → decomposers → denitrifying bacteria); ozone layer & UV globe.
**Animations**: energy flow through trophic levels with 10 % law; biomagnification (DDT concentration rising up a food chain); nitrogen fixation in root nodules; eutrophication (algal bloom → oxygen depletion); ozone depletion by CFCs (Cl radical cycle, simplified).
**Simulations**: (1) **Food-web builder** — remove a species, watch cascading effects; (2) **Ecological pyramid lab** — number, biomass, energy (upright vs inverted examples); (3) **Population growth lab** (NEET) — exponential vs logistic, carrying capacity K, r; predator–prey cycles (labelled teaching model); (4) **Pollution lab** — choose pollutants & sources, see effects on air/water quality indicators (sourced ranges, AQI categories as reference); (5) Waste-sorting game (biodegradable vs non-biodegradable, 3 Rs).
**Missions**: "Save the Sundarbans" — balance human needs and mangrove conservation using in-situ/ex-situ strategies (local relevance for West Bengal learners).
**Assessment**: food-chain construction, 10 % law numericals, pyramid identification, nitrogen-cycle ordering, conservation strategy matching.

## 4. Accuracy specifics
- 10 % law presented as an average approximation (actual transfer efficiencies vary ≈ 5–20 %).
- Pollution values from official agencies with dates; no outdated statistics; regional facts dated and sourced.
- Predator–prey and logistic models labelled idealised.
- Climate/ozone content separated correctly (ozone hole ≠ global warming misconception).
