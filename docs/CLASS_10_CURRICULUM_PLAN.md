## 0. Two Class 10 curricula matter for this app

| Board | Book (2026-27) | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **NCERT / CBSE** | *Science* Class X, rationalised NCF-2005 edition, **Reprint 2026-27** (verified on ncert.nic.in) | Biology chapters: Ch.5 Life Processes · Ch.6 Control and Coordination · Ch.7 How do Organisms Reproduce? · Ch.8 Heredity · (Ch.10 Human Eye — physics chapter) · Ch.13 Our Environment |
| **WBBSE (Madhyamik)** | *Life Science and Environment* Class X (Bengali & English medium) | Ch.1 Control & Coordination in living organisms (1A sensitivity & response in plants, 1B plant hormones, 1C animal hormones, 1D nervous system incl. sense organ – eye, 1E locomotion) · Ch.2 Continuity of Life (2A cell division & cell cycle, 2B reproduction, 2C sexual reproduction in flowering plants, 2D growth & development) · Ch.3 Heredity & some common genetic diseases · Ch.4 Evolution & Adaptation · Ch.5 Environment, its resources & conservation |

The app is bilingual and its owner/users are in West Bengal, so **both tracks are proposed** via a Settings option **"Curriculum: NCERT/CBSE | WBBSE"** (default: owner to decide). The track changes which Class 10 cards, terms, quiz items and chapter references appear; scientific content is shared.

Important: WBBSE places *Life Processes* (nutrition, respiration, excretion, transport) mainly in Class 9, and *cell division, evolution, nitrogen cycle* in Class 10 — the opposite of parts of NCERT. Level labels therefore follow the **selected board**.

## 2. Class 10 feature additions per bay

### 2.1 Cell
- Cell division & cell cycle at **Class 10 depth** (WBBSE 2A): interphase, mitosis stages with animated chromosomes, cytokinesis animal vs plant, meiosis overview & significance; existing A6/AN13/AN14 assets with a simplified caption track.
- Chloroplast & photosynthesis overview card for NCERT Ch.5 (equation 6CO₂ + 12H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ + 6H₂O with light & chlorophyll; textbook form shown).

### 2.2 Tissues
- Xylem & phloem function in transport (NCERT Ch.5): water column / transpiration pull animation (overview), translocation in phloem (overview) — shared TA11.

### 2.3 Digestion — new **"Nutrition" tab**
- Autotrophic vs heterotrophic; **virtual starch-test activity** (variegated leaf, iodine) and **"Is CO₂ needed?"** (KOH bell-jar activity) — classic NCERT activities as interactive experiments.
- Stomata opening/closing (shared TA12).
- Nutrition in Amoeba (pseudopodia, food vacuole) animation; human alimentary canal (existing).

### 2.4 Respiration
- Three pathways of glucose breakdown as an interactive branching diagram (cytoplasm → pyruvate → yeast / muscle / mitochondria), with the textbook energy comparison.
- **Lime-water activity** (CO₂ in exhaled air) and yeast fermentation activity as virtual experiments.
- Exchange of gases in plants (stomata, lenticels — brief).

### 2.5 Circulation
- Blood pressure with **virtual sphygmomanometer** (normal ≈ 120/80 mmHg, textbook value; hypertension note).
- Lymph & lymphatic system; transport in plants link to Tissues.
- Double circulation vs fish (single) / amphibian three-chambered comparison card (NCERT text).

### 2.6 Excretion
- **Artificial kidney (haemodialysis)** interactive model (dialysing fluid, semi-permeable tubing, no reabsorption).
- Excretion in plants (stored in vacuoles, resins/gums, shedding leaves) card.

### 2.7 Brain & Nerves
- Reflex arc (Class 10 centrepiece, corrected timing ≈ 65–150 ms).
- Brain: fore-, mid-, hind-brain with functions table; protection (skull, CSF).
- **New "Eye" sub-tab**: 3D eye (cornea, iris, pupil, lens, ciliary muscles, retina, optic nerve), accommodation animation, myopia/hypermetropia/presbyopia with corrective lens ray diagrams (shared with physics-style ray tracer, simplified).
- **Plant tab**: Mimosa touch response; tropism lab (light direction, gravity, water, chemical — pollen tube) with auxin redistribution animation.
- **Hormones sub-tab**: endocrine gland 3D map; hormone–gland–effect cards; feedback loop simulation (insulin–blood sugar); iodised salt & goitre; growth hormone disorders; plant hormones with demonstrations (auxin in bending, gibberellin in stem elongation, ABA in stomatal closure).

### 2.8 Reproduction
- **Asexual reproduction gallery** with animations (binary/multiple fission, budding, fragmentation, regeneration, spores) + vegetative propagation activities (potato eye, Bryophyllum leaf buds).
- Flower dissection 3D (sepal, petal, stamen, carpel), pollination types, double fertilisation (Class 10 simplified), seed germination time-lapse animation.
- Human reproduction at Class 10 depth (schematic, age-appropriate), reproductive health & contraception overview.
- **"Growth" tab (WBBSE 2D)**: growth curve (sigmoid) simulation, human developmental stages, frog/insect metamorphosis animation.
- Concept: importance of variation from DNA copying (link Heredity).

### 2.9 Heredity
- Mendel's pea experiments mission (monohybrid & dihybrid), traits expressed via proteins (enzyme → pigment example), sex determination in humans (XX/XY) with sampler.
- **Genetic disorders (WBBSE 3B)**: thalassemia (autosomal recessive), haemophilia & colour blindness (X-linked recessive) with pedigree & Punnett demonstrations; respectful, non-stigmatising language; carrier screening mentioned as educational fact only.

## 3. Class 10 assessment
- Board-style question formats: **NCERT/CBSE** (MCQ, assertion–reason, case-based, short/long answer prompts with model answers), **WBBSE Madhyamik** (MCQ, very short answer, match the column, short & descriptive prompts) — written originally, not copied.
- Diagram practice: label-the-diagram on 3D/2D for frequently drawn diagrams (neuron, reflex arc, brain, nephron, heart, alveoli, flower LS, stomata, eye, human reproductive systems schematic).
- Virtual activities (starch test, lime water, fermentation, Mimosa, tropism) each end with an observation table the student fills in.
- Chapter revision sheets (bilingual, printable) per board chapter.

## 4. Accuracy & alignment rules (Class 10 specific)
- Wording follows the selected board's textbook; where WBBSE and NCERT phrase differently, both versions stored and shown per track.
- Textbook simplifications (e.g. "6H₂O" product in photosynthesis equation, "120/80 normal BP") flagged in the conflict register where modern guidelines differ.
- Genetic-disease and reproductive content: respectful, factual, no medical advice.
- Board chapter references (chapter + page) recorded in the evidence ledger for every Class 10 claim; obtain official NCERT X and WBBSE X PDFs/books in Phase 0.
